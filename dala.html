A little about us

Every frame has a feeling.

The Prayag Diaries is a creative photography and film studio inspired by people, places and the moments in between. From personal milestones to brands with a story to tell, we create visuals with care and character.

Our approach is simple: understand your story, notice the little details, and create something that feels unmistakably yours.

The story is yours. The frame is ours.
